# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: 2025-12-30**

### 1. Data Collection
We collect and store the minimum amount of data required to provide our services:
- **User IDs** (Discord Snowflake IDs) to track DKP balances and attendance.
- **Guild IDs** to isolate data between servers.
- **Usage Data**: Including DKP amounts, raid attendance timestamps, auction bids, and audit logs of admin actions.

### 2. Data Usage
The collected data is used solely for the functionality of the bot:
- Tracking and displaying DKP balances.
- Managing raid attendance and awards.
- Conducting auctions and determining winners.
- Providing audit logs for server administrators.

### 3. Data Storage
Data is stored in a secure local database. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

### 4. Data Deletion
- **Server Owners**: Can permanently delete all data associated with their server by running the `!reset_dkp` command. This action is irreversible.
- **Individual Users**: Can request the removal of their data by contacting their server administrator to run `!remove_dkp`

### 5. Changes to Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the bot signifies your acceptance of any changes.
